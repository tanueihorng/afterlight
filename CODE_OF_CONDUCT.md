# Code of conduct

Afterlight is used by people who are frightened about their sight. Some of them will show up here
in the middle of a bad week, describing something they have not been able to explain to anyone else.
That shapes how this project behaves.

## What is expected

**Assume the person is having a worse day than you.** Someone reporting that a screen is unreadable
may have been struggling with it for an hour before they gave up and typed something short and
frustrated. Answer the problem.

**Treat someone's description of their own vision as accurate.** They are the only person who has
seen it. "That is not how that works" is almost never a helpful reply to a patient describing what
they perceive.

**Do not give medical advice.** Not in issues, not in pull request comments, not in a discussion.
Not even when you are a clinician and you are probably right. You have not examined anyone, you
cannot see their history, and a stranger acting on your comment is a real risk. Point at a real
doctor.

**Accessibility feedback outranks feature requests.** Every time. A person who cannot read the app
is not a niche user here; they are the intended user.

**Be careful with other people's records.** Do not ask for someone's export or screenshots to
reproduce a bug. Ask for the shape of the problem. If someone posts personal data by mistake, help
them get it removed rather than quoting it back.

**Disagree about the work, not the person.** Strong opinions about a design decision are welcome and
useful. Comments about the person holding the opinion are not.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Comments about someone's disability, condition, prognosis, or how they cope with it.
- Dismissing a symptom description as imagined, exaggerated, or attention-seeking.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including anything from someone's health record.
- Using this project to market a treatment, a supplement, a clinic or a device.

## Scope

Anywhere the project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space
where you are representing Afterlight.

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory
(<https://github.com/tanueihorng/afterlight/security/advisories/new>) — it is the private channel
this repository has, and it works for conduct reports too. Say what happened. You do not have to
justify why it bothered you.

Reports are read by the maintainer. Your identity is not shared with the person reported without
your agreement.

## What happens

Depending on what happened, and in roughly this order: a private word, a public correction, edited
or removed comments, a temporary block, or a permanent one. Anything involving medical advice given
to a patient, or the disclosure of someone's health information, skips straight to the serious end.

There is one maintainer and no appeals committee. Decisions will be explained.
