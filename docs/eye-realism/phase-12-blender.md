# Phase 12 — the original Blender anatomy, as built

Date 2026-09-13, branch `phase-11-baseline-contract` (phases continue on the same branch while
each keeps its own commits). Everything here is regenerated by
`blender --background --python assets-src/build-anatomy.py` after
`app/scripts/anatomy/export-anatomy-data.mjs`; the `.blend`, the binary export and the renders
are committed outputs, reproducible from those two commands.

## What was authored

Every static structure from the contract, each a watertight solid assembled from regular grid
patches with real wall thickness:

- **sclera** — ellipsoidal shell (0.93 ml validated volume) with a circular limbal aperture and
  a scleral canal built around the disc axis; thickness 1.0 posterior → 0.5 equator → 0.4 limbal.
- **cornea** — anterior aspheric cap (R 7.8, Q −0.26) over a posterior conic solved at build
  time to the measured 0.55/0.67 mm central/peripheral thickness (solved Q ≈ −1.05).
- **iris** — conical annulus resting on the lens, geometric aperture fixed at 0.7 mm (the visible
  pupil stays runtime-painted), painter UVs radial on the anterior face.
- **lens** — biconvex, Ø9 × 4 mm, caps' conics solved from the measured central radii.
- **ciliary_body, zonules** (36 fibres), **retina** (0.25 mm wall, fundus-painter UVs on the
  inner surface), **choroid** (×3 labelled magnification, from `params.json`).
- **optic_nerve** — core + sheath along the disc axis with a slight sinuous bow, flat-capped;
  **nerve_head** dome with a physiological cup filling the retinal disc opening.
- **muscles** — four recti: superellipse straps (belly ~10 × 3.8 mm) from the apex ring to the
  spiral of Tillaux insertions, tendon slot split by row, feet wrapping onto the globe wall.
- **vessels** — the shared seeded tree, exported from `vessels.ts` in eye-local millimetres, as
  study-only tubes on the inner retina. The browser regrows these at runtime; nothing is baked.

The iris bake pipeline (`build-eye.py`) runs unchanged inside the build; its sculpt and receiver
are authoring apparatus and are hidden from renders. `preview-eye.py` was removed as superseded.

## Validation

- Blender-side, per build: finite coordinates, zero boundary edges for every solid (open tubes
  are none — all tubes are capped), positive signed volume after `recalc_face_normals`. Sclera
  volume 0.94 ml.
- Browser-side, in `app/src/engine/anatomy/model.test.ts` (runs in CI without Blender): all 15
  structures decode, finite and inside orbital bounds, **zero boundary edges after the weld**
  for every solid, index/slot consistency, disc nasal-posterior with the fovea on-axis, retina
  strictly inside the sclera, iris aperture 0.55–0.85 mm reaching past 5.5 mm, binary ≤ 2.6 MB,
  and the shared retina radius matches the vessel projection constant. 9 tests, all passing.
- `npm run verify` clean: 554 tests, assets hash-verified, budgets hold
  (initial JS 81.0/120 KB, Visualize lazy chunk + embedded data within the 12 MB cap).

## The four studies (inspected, not assumed)

`assets-src/renders/study-*.png`, Cycles, neutral studio:

- **Cutaway** — the contract composition: corneal dome to screen upper-right, cut plane facing
  the viewer, retinal bowl readable, biconvex lens behind the iris with the ciliary body, the
  layered wall edge (sclera/choroid/retina) visible around the rim, optic nerve and muscle cone
  posterio-inferiorly. Structural items 1, 2, 3, 5 of the handoff hold here.
- **Exterior** — white globe, corneal aperture with the iris behind it, the red fundus reflex
  through the pupil, muscle insertion feet near the limbus in the spiral's order.
- **Cornea close-up** — limbal ring, corneal dome over the iris plane; the study iris is neutral
  geometry (the browser paints it), which is expected and recorded.
- **Retina close-up** — the bowl interior with the four arcades converging on the disc and the
  nerve-head dome; the avascular centre stays clear.

Honest gaps visible in the studies, carried to phase 13 rather than hidden: the choroid's cut
band reads dark in the crevice shadow (browser materials will light the rim explicitly); broad
shading bands on the bowl interior mark the polar-cap normal seams (cosmetic, browser normals
are computed per-vertex and soften them); the study iris is unpainted by design.

## Handoff to phase 13

- `app/src/engine/anatomy/model.ts` is the loader; `decodeAnatomy(bin, manifest)` returns
  per-structure typed arrays with slot-tagged triangles (`retina_inner` vs cut slots, muscle
  belly vs tendon by row range). A Three r180 adapter converts them to `BufferGeometry` with
  `computeVertexNormals()`; the legacy explorer gets the same decoder embedded via the
  `sync-eye-assets.mjs` marked block in phase 15.
- The manifest carries `disc3d`, `fovea3d`, `retinaInnerRadiusMm`, `layerMagnification` — the
  anchors the renderer needs without re-deriving anything.
- Slice caps (phase 13) compute from the decoded grids directly; the exporter's grid regularity
  is what makes that possible.
