# Product Video Design Framework

A product-agnostic reference based on [“Make your product videos look expensive (Apple Framework)” by leo](https://x.com/leomeethewoo/status/2103529310208606701).

Accessed: 26 September 2026.

This document summarises the article in its original order and records observations from its embedded examples. It is not a verbatim transcription or an official Apple guideline. No product-specific adaptation has been added.

## 1. Branding — premium storytelling

Build a recognisable identity through deliberate, repeatable decisions about type, backgrounds, colour and music. Limit the visual vocabulary. Remove effects that undermine the intended character; technical complexity alone does not create quality.

## 2. Design

Resolve still compositions before animating. Give each shot a clear message, generous space and an obvious focal subject. Backgrounds should support that subject. Establish consistent visual rules and storyboard the sequence before production.

## 3. Animation

### Smoothness

Shape acceleration and deceleration with easing; overlap movements rather than mechanically completing everything in sequence.

### Dynamic transitions

Connect scenes through purposeful movement. Use direct cuts when they serve the message.

### Adaptive rhythm

Vary pace: accelerate, slow down and hold according to the content.

## 4. Music choice

Choose energy and genre for the audience. The author's suggested tempo associations are:

| Tempo | Suggested character |
| --- | --- |
| 60–80 BPM | Stately and cinematic |
| 90–110 BPM | Relaxed and assured |
| 115–123 BPM | Refined but energetic |
| Above that range | Greater intensity; suitability depends on the project |

These are creative heuristics, not universal rules.

## 5. Sound design

Audit the finished mix. Remove sounds that distract, overpower or fail to help understanding. One source sentence is ambiguous; this summary follows the surrounding paragraph's emphasis on restraint.

The five sections above paraphrase the [original article](https://x.com/leomeethewoo/status/2103529310208606701).

## Accuracy boundaries

- The full article was read. Playback was opened for all seven clips and selected visual states were inspected.
- This was sampled browser inspection, not frame-by-frame reconstruction. An attempted media download timed out; original video files and extracted frames are not included.
- Exact typefaces, colour values, spacing, easing curves, frame rates, resolutions, codecs, track names and audio levels were not established.
- Do not treat the examples' different palettes as one mandatory design system.
- X's surrounding interface and player borders are not automatically part of the videos' art direction.
- The reference does not establish website scroll behaviour, hover states, responsive breakpoints or reduced-motion handling. Those require separate decisions if the framework is later applied to an interactive experience.
- No new storyboard, brand palette, implementation stack, asset-generation prompt or numerical animation recipe has been invented for this document.

## Asset reference

Use the linked article to revisit its embedded examples. No third-party media, font or soundtrack is supplied as a reusable production asset by this Markdown file. A later implementation should identify its own product assets and delivery requirements separately.
